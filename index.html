About the data. Compiled from CCPR's Chinland Monitoring Briefing, which draws, as far as accessible, on news media reports, statements of the relevant organizations and information from local residents. Actual figures on the ground may be higher. Counts show what was recorded, not complete totals.

Locations use MIMU Place Codes; boundaries are MIMU township boundaries and do not imply endorsement. How the data is collected and checked.
